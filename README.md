# 📊 Excel Sales Dashboard: Executive Summary & Profitability

An interactive Excel dashboard that gives a quick, executive-level view of **revenue, gross profit, margins, orders and units sold** across European markets, product categories, sales channels and time periods.

## 🎯 Objective

To turn raw sales data into a single, easy-to-read dashboard that helps answer key business questions:

- How much revenue and profit are we generating?
- Which countries and regions perform best?
- Which sales channels contribute the most?
- How do revenue and profit change month by month?
- Which product categories and quarters drive results?

## 📈 Dashboard Components

| Visual | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **KPI Cards** | Revenue, GP, GP Margin, Units Sold, Orders and COGS at a glance |
| **Line Chart** | Monthly trend of Total Revenue vs Total Gross Profit (Jan–Dec) |
| **Bar Chart** | Revenue by country, ranked from highest to lowest |
| **Pie Chart** | Revenue share by sales channel (Corporate, Marketplace, Online Store, Retail Partners) |
| **Donut Chart** | Revenue share by region (Central, Northern, Southern, Western Europe) |
| **Map Chart** | Geographic view of total revenue across Europe |
| **Slicers** | Interactive filters for Product Category, Country and Quarter |

## 🔍 Key Insights

- **Top markets:** The United Kingdom (₹74K) and Germany (₹70K) lead in revenue, followed by France (₹55K), Italy (₹50K) and Spain (₹47K).
- **Lowest market:** Ireland has the lowest revenue at ₹17K.
- **Best channel:** The Online Store contributes the largest share at **40%**, followed by Corporate (28%), Retail Partners (18%) and Marketplace (14%).
- **Regional split:** Western Europe (31%) and Central Europe (30%) together account for over 60% of revenue.
- **Seasonality:** Revenue peaks in **June (₹70K)**, with another strong month in **November (₹66K)**. February is the lowest point (₹36K).
- **Profitability:** Gross profit stays steady through the year (roughly ₹17K–₹31K per month), supporting a healthy overall margin of nearly 46%.

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Download or open the Excel file using the link above.
2. Use the **Product Category**, **Country** and **Quarter** slicers on the right to filter the dashboard.
3. Click multiple slicer items (hold `Ctrl`) to compare selections.
4. Use **Clear Filter** on a slicer to reset the view.
